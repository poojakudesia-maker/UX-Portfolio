About

Twenty years making high-stakes software usable, accessible and buildable.

I'm Pooja Kudesia, Sr. Manager of User Experience at Aristocrat Technologies. I've spent my career on products where a confusing screen turns into a compliance breach, a lost transaction or an operator mistake on a casino floor.

How I lead

I sit between product strategy and engineering delivery. My job is to turn a business goal into a set of decisions a team can build, test and defend, and then make sure those decisions survive contact with sprint planning.

  • Standards before screens

    A governed design system with accessibility built into components beats auditing every release after the fact.

  • Designers who can ship

    I hire and grow people who understand the code. It shortens handoff and raises the quality bar on both sides.

  • Co-create with the people at risk

    Operators, compliance and engineering are in the room early, because they're the ones who pay for a wrong workflow.

  • Measure the outcome

    Every programme has a number attached: delivery time, retention, task completion or satisfaction.

Scale and domains

I've led teams across India, the UK and the US, including directing 20 engineers and developers on-site in London. I've worked in casino gaming, banking and financial services, health-tech e-commerce, engineering and construction, and telecom. All of them regulated or operations-critical.
